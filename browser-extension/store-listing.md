## Detailed description

Buddy AI is your emotional companion for the age of AI chat. It floats beside you on ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini — reading the emotional tone of conversations locally on your device and reacting with a living orb that breathes, listens, and gently guides you.

Unlike passive chat tools, Buddy remembers your mood patterns, celebrates milestones, and offers subjectivity when negative self-talk appears. It never sends raw conversation text to our servers unless you explicitly opt in. Your emotions stay on your device.

Buddy AI also acts as a hallucination shield — surfacing concern when responses feel off, helping you pause before trusting everything you read. Free forever for the core companion; upgrade to Pro for advanced analytics, personalized blessings, and compliance-ready reports.
