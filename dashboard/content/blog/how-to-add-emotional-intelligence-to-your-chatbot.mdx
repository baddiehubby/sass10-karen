## Step 1: Get your license key

Create a free Buddie AI account and generate a Buddy license in your dashboard.

## Step 2: Paste the script

Embed the widget before `</body>`:

```html
<script
  src="https://buddieai.com/widget/livingface-widget.js"
  data-hq-key="YOUR_LICENSE_KEY"
  data-hq-api="https://buddieai.com"
  async
></script>
```

## Step 3: Open analytics

Visit `/dashboard/analytics` to view sentiment trends, mood timelines, and top joy/concern words.
