## Why this matters

We modeled **1 million+** anonymized interactions to understand how users emotionally respond to AI assistants.

## High-level mood split

- Neutral: 44%
- Happy: 27%
- Thinking: 15%
- Concerned: 10%
- Excited: 4%

## Key takeaway

Most teams only see resolution rates. Buddie AI helps you see emotional quality, so you can humanize your AI and reduce avoidable escalation.
