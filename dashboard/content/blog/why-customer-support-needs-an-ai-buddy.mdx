## The support blind spot

Traditional dashboards track tickets and time-to-resolution, but they miss emotional drift.

## What Buddie reveals

- Early warning of concern-heavy sessions
- Repeated triggers by domain, flow, or release
- Before-and-after emotional impact of product changes

## Bottom line

When your AI feels emotionally legible, support becomes calmer, faster, and more human.
